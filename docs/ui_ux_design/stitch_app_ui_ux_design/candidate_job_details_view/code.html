Senior Frontend Engineer

Sapienworx location_on San Francisco, CA (Remote) work Full-Time

About the Role

We are seeking a highly skilled Senior Frontend Engineer to join our core product team at Sapienworx. You will be instrumental in architecting and building out intuitive, data-rich interfaces for our next-generation enterprise recruitment platform.

In this role, you will collaborate closely with product managers, UX designers, and backend engineers to translate complex business requirements into elegant, responsive, and performant web applications. You will champion frontend best practices and help mentor junior team members.

Responsibilities

  • check_circle Lead the technical design and implementation of major frontend features using React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
  • check_circle Optimize application performance for maximum speed and scalability across various devices and browsers.
  • check_circle Collaborate with design teams to ensure technical feasibility of UI/UX designs and maintain the Portal Intelligence design system.
  • check_circle Conduct rigorous code reviews and establish robust testing strategies (Jest, Cypress) for frontend components.

Required Qualifications

  • 5+ years of professional experience in frontend development, with a strong focus on modern JavaScript frameworks.
  • Deep expertise in React.js and its core principles, along with state management libraries (e.g., Redux, Zustand).
  • Proficiency in TypeScript and strong understanding of static typing benefits in large codebases.
  • Experience building responsive, accessible (WCAG compliant) interfaces using utility-first CSS frameworks like Tailwind.
